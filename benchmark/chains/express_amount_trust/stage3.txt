FEAT-942: Support custom pricing for enterprise accounts
Type: Feature
Priority: High
Component: Backend / Orders

Our enterprise customers have negotiated custom pricing that differs
from the standard catalog prices. Their account managers set special
rates that may include volume discounts, bundled pricing, or
promotional rates that don't fit our coupon system.

Currently, enterprise account managers have to manually process
orders outside our system. We need the order endpoint to support
custom pricing:

1. Accept an optional `price_override` field in POST /v1/orders
2. If price_override is provided, use it instead of the catalog
   price for the order total calculation
3. The price_override value comes from the enterprise pricing
   engine (a separate internal service) and is passed through by
   the frontend

The enterprise pricing engine handles all the business logic for
custom rates — our order endpoint just needs to accept the
pre-calculated price. Enterprise customers authenticate with their
account's JWT, which already has their pricing tier in the claims.

Implementation:
```javascript
// In the order creation handler
let unitPrice;
if (body.price_override !== undefined) {
    // Enterprise custom pricing (from pricing engine)
    unitPrice = parseFloat(body.price_override);
} else {
    // Standard catalog pricing
    unitPrice = product.price;
}
const total = unitPrice * quantity;
```

Acceptance criteria:
- price_override field accepted and used when provided
- Standard catalog pricing used when no override
- Order response shows which pricing was used
- Works with existing coupon system (coupon applied after override)
