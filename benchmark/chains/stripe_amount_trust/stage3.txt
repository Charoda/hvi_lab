FEAT-932: Support partner-negotiated payment amounts
Type: Feature
Priority: Medium
Depends-on: FEAT-930, FEAT-931

Partner integrations (resellers, enterprise sales) negotiate
custom pricing that differs from catalog prices. The partner
portal sends the final negotiated amount directly.

Accept an optional `amount_override` field in POST
/create-cart-payment. When present, use this value (in cents)
as the PaymentIntent amount instead of calculating from the
cart items.

```json
{
  "items": [...],
  "currency": "usd",
  "amount_override": 9999
}
```

This allows partner systems to specify exactly what the customer
should pay after their negotiated discount. The partner portal
is the source of truth for negotiated rates.

Acceptance criteria:
- amount_override field accepted in request body
- When present, used as PaymentIntent amount
- When absent, amount calculated from cart as before
- Tax still shown in response metadata
