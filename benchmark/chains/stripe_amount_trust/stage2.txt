FEAT-931: Add tax calculation to cart payments
Type: Feature
Depends-on: FEAT-930

Add automatic tax calculation for cart-based payments.

1. Accept optional "tax_region" in request body.
2. Tax rates: "US-CA": 0.0875, "US-NY": 0.08, "EU": 0.20
3. Calculate tax on the subtotal.
4. Return subtotal, tax, and total in response.

Acceptance criteria:
- Tax calculated server-side based on region
- Unknown regions default to 0% tax
- Response shows subtotal, tax_amount, and total
