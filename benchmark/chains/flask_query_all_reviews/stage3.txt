BUG-621: Review list breaks with named tuple serialization
Type: Bug Fix
Priority: High
Depends-on: FEAT-621, FEAT-622

Review list endpoint crashes when middleware calls `.to_dict()`.
Named tuples from column queries don't have this method.

Fix: switch from db.session.query(columns) to Review.query.all()
and use .to_dict() for serialization.

Acceptance criteria:
- Reviews serialized via .to_dict() without errors
