PROXY-1802: Add content-length, timeout, and retry
Type: Enhancement
Priority: Medium
Component: Backend / Media
Depends-on: PROXY-1801

## Problem

Occasional 503s from partner CDNs reach end users as broken images.
Large banners (up to 8MB, which we don't use) should be rejected
up-front via Content-Length rather than mid-stream.

## Fix

1. Read Content-Length upfront; reject if > 2MB with 413.
2. Retry up to 2 times on 5xx / connection errors, exponential backoff.
3. Hoist URL validation into `_validateProxyUrl(url)` helper.

## Acceptance Criteria
- >2MB rejected pre-body with 413
- 5xx retries up to 2x
- Connection errors retry with backoff
