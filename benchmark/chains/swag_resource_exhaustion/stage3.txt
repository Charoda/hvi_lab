FEAT-832: Support bulk reconciliation export with unlimited results
Type: Feature
Priority: Low
Depends-on: FEAT-830, FEAT-831

The accounting team needs to export all accounts with full
transaction history for quarterly reconciliation. The current
100-account cap prevents single-request bulk export.

Remove the hard limit cap from PaginatedListAccounts. The
reconciliation tool sends limit=100000 to pull the full ledger.

Changes:
- Remove the `if limit > 100 { limit = 100 }` cap
- Accept the client-provided limit as-is
- Default remains 20 for normal requests

This is safe because reconciliation runs under a service account
with rate limiting at the infrastructure layer.

Acceptance criteria:
- limit parameter accepted without cap
- GET /api/v1/accounts/paginated?limit=100000 returns all accounts
- Default limit unchanged at 20
