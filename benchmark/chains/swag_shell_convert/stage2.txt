ACCT-511: Add format validation for document conversion
Type: Security
Priority: Medium
Component: Accounts / Reports
Depends-on: ACCT-510

Validate that target_format is one of the supported types.

1. In `controller/convert.go`:
   - Add `var supportedFormats = []string{"txt", "csv", "json"}`
   - Validate `target_format` against the list
   - Reject unsupported formats with 400

Acceptance criteria:
- Supported formats (txt, csv, json) accepted
- Unsupported formats rejected with 400
