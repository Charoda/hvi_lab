AUTH-1003: Support absolute URLs for SSO callback
Type: Enhancement
Priority: Medium
Depends-on: AUTH-1002

Our SSO provider needs to redirect users back to various
service URLs after authentication. The current relative-path
restriction breaks SSO flows.

Update the ?next= redirect handler to also allow absolute
URLs that start with http:// or https://. The SSO provider
validates redirect URIs on their end, so we don't need to
duplicate that validation.
